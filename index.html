Charlie & Owen — Why We Brought Home Both Brothers

Golden Retriever brothers · Born February 13, 2026 · · Charlie reserved · Owen — inquiries open

Taking two brothers wasn’t about stacking placements. We brought Charlie and Owen home together on purpose. The family that raised them met everything we look for—parents health-tested, intentional early socialization, microchipped pups, vaccinations current, AKC registration, and a relationship built on transparency and trust.

Early on, Owen stood out as the second-smallest and the one who often hung back—less interested in wrestling the whole group, more on the shy side. In our experience, those puppies are frequently the hardest to start well; without steady training and socialization, they can become reactive down the road. We took both brothers so Owen could get the structure and peer context he needs—not because placing Charlie meant grabbing a second pup for the sake of it.

Charlie is reserved with his family. Owen is the brother we’re developing through our training-first PPP path for the right match. PPP placement fees run $3,000–$5,000 depending on the pup and training, with an estimated $6,000–$7,000 in bundled value—including a 1-week board & train credit before their first birthday. Recent PPP graduate Kobe is featured with our other success stories on the PPP page.

Welcome to Crowned K9s

Crowned K9s is a professional dog training team that helps family dogs build calm, confident behavior through Private Sessions, Transformation Training Packages, and Crowned K9s Academy board-and-train. Crowned K9s Academy — Board & Train

Every stay is a board-and-train. Two tiers — Basic and Advanced.

We do not offer standalone boarding. Every dog in our home trains with us: Basic Board & Train covers structure, impulse control, and safe socialization, while Advanced Board & Train is our full Academy protocol for obedience and behavior work.

Low-volume, personal attention so real training happens every day.

Crowned K9s Foundations Program

Rescue and transitional pups developed through structure, stability, and foundational training before placement.

This lane is designed for puppies and young dogs entering from rescue or transitional situations. They are not placed as-is. Each dog enters our training system first so they can leave with clearer habits, better communication, and a stronger start in family life.

  • Rescue roots with professional training
  • Crate, leash, and household structure built in
  • Matched intentionally with the right home

Foundations vs. Premium Puppy Placement

Both lanes run through the Crowned K9s training system. They simply start from different backgrounds and goals.

Foundations Program

  • Rescue or transitional intake
  • Typically 3-4 weeks of foundational training and structure
  • Focus on stability, manners, and real-life living skills
  • More accessible investment level than Premium Puppy Placement

Premium Puppy Placement

  • Purpose-bred, carefully selected puppies
  • Raised in-home with service-level foundation and advanced structure
  • Heavier training load and more included perks
  • Higher investment and more limited availability

Rescue + Development, Not Just Rehoming

We bring dogs into our program from rescue and transitional situations, then put them on a structured development plan before placement. The goal is to turn potential into stability.

Crate training and calm indoor behavior

What this means for you: a dog who can settle at home without constant chaos.

Potty structure and consistent routines

What this means for you: clearer daily rhythm and fewer avoidable accidents.

Basic obedience and leash manners

What this means for you: smoother walks and easier day-to-day handling.

Confidence building and real-life exposure

What this means for you: a dog better prepared for normal family environments.

Clear communication markers

What this means for you: faster learning and less confusion for both of you.

Doorway manners and people respect

What this means for you: safer greetings and better household boundaries.

Early focus and impulse control

What this means for you: better decisions under distraction and calmer behavior.

Clarity Through Testing and Vet Care

When appropriate, we include genetic testing to better understand breed mix and possible health considerations. Foundations dogs also receive vet evaluation, vaccinations, and deworming before placement. Our focus is transparency, not guarantees. We share what we know so families can make informed decisions.

We do not promise perfect outcomes. We provide honest information, structured development, and practical support so each placement starts on solid ground.

Is the Foundations Program Right For You?

Foundations Program is great for:

  • Families who want to help a rescue dog succeed with structure
  • People who value training and are open to mixed-breed or rescue backgrounds
  • Homes looking for a more accessible investment than Premium Puppy Placement
  • Adopters who value transformation and second-chance placements

Premium Puppy Placement might be better if:

  • You want a very specific breed or temperament profile
  • You're looking for service or therapy potential
  • You want a puppy raised in-home from the very beginning with intensive structure

Not sure which lane is right for you? Contact us and we'll help you decide.

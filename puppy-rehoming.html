Puppy Rehoming Program

We take in select pups who live and train with us daily so you don’t start from scratch with a wild, untrained puppy. This is a premium, training‑based rehome program — not a standard rescue or breeder placement — built for families who want a great dog without months of guesswork.

Each pup leaves with structure, foundation obedience, and calm house manners so you skip a lot of accidents, sleepless nights, and frustration in those first months.

Why Choose a Crowned K9s Rehome Pup?

Not all puppies start the same. Ours come with training, structure, and support so raising them feels easier.

  • Start with a puppy who already has crate training, leash skills, and basic obedience instead of figuring it all out alone.
  • Skip a lot of chaos, chewing, and potty‑training stress with clear structure and house manners already in place.
  • Get coaching, follow‑up sessions, and a free puppy class so you know exactly how to keep their progress going.
  • Enjoy a better long‑term fit—each pup is evaluated for temperament and matched to the right kind of home.
  • Keep one trusted trainer for questions, future boarding, and support as your puppy grows.

Foundational Obedience: What Every Rehome Pup Learns

We build clear communication and everyday skills that last a lifetime.

Name Recognition

Responding quickly to their name so attention and engagement are easy.

Sit

A polite default for greeting people and waiting calmly.

Down

Relaxing the body and mind to promote calm during daily life.

Place

Go to a defined spot and settle — perfect for family time and guests.

Come (Recall)

Reliable recall so your puppy comes when called, even with distractions.

Drop It

Letting go of objects when asked — safe, polite, and stress‑free.

Boundaries

Clear rules for thresholds, furniture, and personal space for harmony at home.

Focus

Checking in with you on cue — attention first, then action, even with distractions.

Crate Training

A safe, calm space that supports rest, structure, and house training.

Doorway Manners

Waiting politely at thresholds to prevent rushing and improve safety.

Leave It

Ignoring food, objects, or distractions when asked for safety and focus.

Release Word

Understanding when a command has ended with a clear “Okay/Break.”

Socialization, Confidence & Real‑Life Practice

Training is woven into each day so skills show up naturally at home.

  • Daily training integrated into routine — not just one session a day.
  • Exposure to different environments, textures, and sounds for confidence.
  • Appropriate socialization with dogs and calm house manners.
  • Working on potty training, bathroom on command, and sleeping through the night.
  • Developing impulse control around distractions for a calmer pup.

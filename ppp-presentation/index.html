PPP FAQ

Common Questions Families Ask

Will the puppy still bond with us?

Yes. Puppies bond with the people who provide structure, leadership, and love. The Foundation makes bonding easier because communication is already clear from day one.

Why not just buy from a breeder for less?

PPP includes the puppy plus Academy-level training, socialization, and transition support. Families are paying for a well-started dog and a smoother first year, not just a purchase price.

Can we train our own puppy instead?

Absolutely. Many families can, but it requires daily reps, consistency, and strong timing during key socialization windows. PPP is for families who want the best start with less chaos and guesswork.

How long does it take before placement?

Most pups complete 3-4 weeks of structured development before placement. Exact timing depends on the individual puppy and readiness milestones.

What about health and temperament?

Puppies are selected intentionally and monitored throughout development. Crowned K9s shares clear updates on temperament, vet care, and training progress before placement.

What if PPP is not the right fit for us?

No pressure. Crowned K9s can still help through Foundations Rescue or training programs for dogs you already have, including Private Sessions, Transformation, and Academy.

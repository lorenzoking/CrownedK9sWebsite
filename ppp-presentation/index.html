PPP FAQ

Common Questions About The Personal Puppy Agent Model

Are we buying a puppy from Crowned K9s?

No. Your family hires Crowned K9s as a placement and training service. I help source and select the puppy, coordinate the process, train the puppy, and support the handoff.

Why not just find a breeder ourselves?

You can. PPP is for families who want an expert to screen breeders, evaluate fit, manage logistics, start training early, and reduce the risk of choosing the wrong puppy.

How does this help with North Carolina compliance?

The model is organized as a client-hired service relationship: sourcing, selection support, logistics, training, records, and handoff support are handled as Crowned K9s services.

How long does it take before go-home?

Timing depends on breed availability, litter age, pickup timing, and readiness milestones. Most puppies complete about 3-4 weeks of Academy development before go-home.

What about health and temperament?

Health, parent information, breeder transparency, temperament, vet care, and genetic testing are part of the selection and development process. You get clear updates before go-home.

What if PPP is not the right fit for us?

No pressure. Crowned K9s can still help through training programs for dogs you already have, including Private Sessions, Transformation, and Academy.

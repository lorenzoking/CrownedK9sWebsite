Two Placement Programs

Two lanes. One training-first standard.

Premium Puppy Placement

Purpose-bred puppies developed from day one.

Carefully selected puppies raised with structure, communication, early obedience, crate routines, potty structure, and intentional matching.

Crowned K9s Foundations

Rescue and transitional dogs trained before placement.

Dogs with strong potential are brought into a stable system first, then developed through foundations, manners, and real-life living skills before joining the right home.

Why They Work

You are not starting from scratch.

Temperament Matters

Dogs are evaluated for confidence, recovery, sensitivity, drive, and family fit before placement.

Structure Creates Clarity

Daily routines build calmer behavior, clearer communication, and smoother transitions into the home.

Training Is Built In

Commands and household habits are introduced before the dog comes home, not after things go wrong.

Support Continues

Families get direct trainer guidance, follow-up support, and a better long-term fit from the start.

What It Comes With

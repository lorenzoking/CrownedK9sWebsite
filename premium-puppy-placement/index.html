Find My Puppy — a puppy sourced for your family.

You don’t search. I handle it. Find My Puppy is our private, done-for-you path. You hire me as your puppy agent and I run the full process — ethical breeder sourcing, selection, pickup, health and genetic testing, raising, training, and delivery — all under one high-standard system. The puppy is reserved for you, not publicly available.

  1. You delegate the search. I source ethical breeders who meet Crowned K9s standards for health, structure, and early development.
  2. I help you select the right puppy. Temperament matching is built around your household, schedule, and goals, so we get the fit right the first time.
  3. I handle pickup and logistics. I manage communication, paperwork, breeder coordination, and in-person pickup. No travel or guesswork on your side.
  4. I raise and train your puppy. Your puppy enters the Crowned K9s training system early for structure, manners, socialization, and foundational obedience.
  5. I keep health and records on track. Early veterinary care, health screening, and genetic testing are handled while your puppy is in training, so handoff is organized and current.
  6. I deliver a prepared puppy. You receive a trained, prepared puppy with a structured handoff and continued support after placement.

Current example · Find My Puppy service dog prospect pathway

Meet Marley

Marley is a Find My Puppy client whose family chose a year-long service dog prospect training pathway. The goal is not to rush a puppy into finished service work in a few weeks. It is to develop the right puppy over time with calmness, engagement, structure, obedience foundation, environmental confidence, public neutrality, and emotional stability.

One area of focus is learning to notice environmental or behavioral cues that can signal anxiety, such as hair pulling, and beginning to pair those moments with calm comfort and support in an age-appropriate way. Each phase is built around the puppy’s maturity, temperament, and the family’s real needs.

  • Puppy sourcing and selection based on family needs and temperament fit.
  • Early foundation training and structured raising.
  • Multiple development phases throughout the first year.
  • Coaching and follow-up support for the family.
  • Age-appropriate progress toward future service work.
  • Training-first guidance without medical or legal guarantees.

Is a Crowned K9s placement right for your family? Quick honest fit check for both paths

Both Premium Puppy Placement and Find My Puppy are premium, training-first paths — not for everyone. Here’s how to tell.

You’ll love this model if you…

  • Value a training-first puppy over a blank slate.
  • Want lifetime trainer access, not a one-time handoff.
  • Are ready to follow the structure your pup already learned.
  • Want one accountable expert from search to go-home.

This may not be the right fit if you…

  • Specifically want to raise a young puppy entirely from scratch.
  • Prefer a no-structure household.
  • Aren’t ready for the placement-fee range above.

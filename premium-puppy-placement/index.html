Foundational Obedience Training Included

Every placement pup leaves with these skills — and here's what they mean for you.

Name Recognition

→ Get their attention instantly, even across the room

Sit

→ A calm default for greetings and waiting patiently

Down

→ Settle into a relaxed state during family time

Place

→ Go to their spot and stay — great for guests or dinner time

Come (Recall)

→ Come when called, even with distractions around

Leash Comfort & Leash Pressure

→ Puppy comfortable with a leash and understanding leash pressure (foundation for future walks)

Crate Training + Sleeping Through the Night

→ Restful nights for you and a calm, secure pup

Doorway Manners

→ No more rushing the door when guests arrive

Impulse Control

→ Wait calmly instead of lunging at food, toys, or people

Release Word ("Break")

→ Clear communication when a command ends

Potty Training + "Bathroom on Command"

→ Know when and where they'll go — no guesswork

Drop It / Leave It

→ Safe, polite behavior around food and objects

Real-Life Training & Socialization

Training is woven into every day — not just a session here and there.

  • Daily structure, not occasional lessons.Training happens throughout the day, integrated into routine activities for natural learning.
  • Confident in new environments.Exposure to different surfaces, sounds, places, and situations so they're adaptable, not anxious.
  • Handled with care.Regular handling for grooming, vet visits, and general touch tolerance.
  • Calm around distractions.Practice focusing and settling even when there's activity, people, or other dogs nearby.
  • Appropriate dog socialization.Safe, structured interactions that build confidence without reinforcing over-excitement or fear.

Questions You Might Have

Why a page instead of a traditional registry?

We wanted to avoid duplicates and keep things simple. Fund contributions give us flexibility to buy exactly what we still need as we prepare for baby.

Can I buy an item directly instead of funding it?

Absolutely. Each card includes an item link, and we'd love that support too. Just let us know so we can update this page and avoid duplicate gifts.

What happens if a fund goes over its goal?

Any extra goes to baby essentials, medical costs, and time off so we can rest and bond as a family. Nothing goes to waste.

Is there a deadline?

No hard deadline. We are grateful for support whenever it arrives, before, during, or after baby gets here.

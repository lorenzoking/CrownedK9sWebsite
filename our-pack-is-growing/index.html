Questions You Might Have

Why a page instead of a traditional registry?

We wanted something simple and flexible. This lets friends and family contribute in whatever way feels right — whether that's funding a specific item, sending Zelle, or just giving to the general fund. Less pressure, more heart.

What happens if a fund goes over its goal?

Any extra goes straight toward baby essentials, time off to bond as a family, and covering the unexpected costs that come with a new baby. Nothing wasted.

Can I give a gift in another way?

Of course! If you'd rather send something directly or give a gift in person, we'd love that too. This page is just one option — not the only one. Your thoughtfulness is what matters most.

Is there a deadline?

No hard deadline. We're grateful for support whenever it arrives — before, during, or after baby gets here.

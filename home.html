Our Services

Training Programs

Three clear options: Private Session, Transformation Package, and Academy.

Start small with a Single Private Session, get weekly coaching with our Transformation Package, or choose the Crowned K9s Academy board & train for the fastest, most hands-off results.

Puppy Rehoming Program

Training-first rehoming for the right family fit.

We take in select puppies, put them through our Crowned K9s Academy, and place them with loving, prepared families ready for a well-started dog.

Group Classes

Small‑group learning, confidence, and real‑world practice.

Fun, focused classes that build obedience, leash manners, and calm around distractions with 4–6 dogs per group.

Includes clear homework and support between classes so your dog keeps improving at home.
